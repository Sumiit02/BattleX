Privacy Policy

Effective date: April 24, 2026. This policy explains how BATTLE-X collects, uses, stores, and protects user information for account creation, tournament operations, payments, and support.

1. Data We Collect

  • Account details: username, email, phone, and login metadata.
  • Tournament details: registrations, team details, mode selections, and match-related records.
  • Payment details: transaction references and status metadata from payment gateways.

2. Why We Use Data

  • To provide tournament services, verify participation, and process payouts.
  • To prevent fraud, enforce anti-cheat policies, and resolve disputes.
  • To meet legal, tax, audit, and payment-gateway compliance obligations.

3. Cashfree Required Disclosures

When Cashfree or similar gateways are used, BATTLE-X shares only required fields for payment processing, verification, risk control, reconciliation, and refund handling. Sensitive card data is handled by the payment provider and not stored directly by BATTLE-X.

4. Data Retention and Security

Records are retained only as long as needed for operations, legal duties, fraud prevention, and accounting. We use reasonable technical and administrative safeguards, but no online system can guarantee absolute security.

5. User Rights

You may request profile corrections and account-related information using official support channels. Certain records may be retained when legally required.

6. Third-Party Services

Our platform may use third-party services for authentication, infrastructure, analytics, and payment operations. Their policies apply to their data processing activities.
