Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about joining events, payments, refunds, and account support.

How do I join a tournament?

Go to the event page, choose a mode, complete registration details, and confirm payment if required.

When are room details shared?

Room credentials are shared according to event timing and check-in policy before match start.

How do payouts work?

Payouts are processed after match verification and anti-cheat review, then sent to approved payout channels.

Can I get a refund?

Refund eligibility depends on event state, policy conditions, and transaction logs. See the refund policy page for details.

How do I raise a dispute?

Open a support ticket from your account and submit match details with evidence as early as possible.

How do I contact support?

Use the Contact Us page or support ticket route available on the platform.
