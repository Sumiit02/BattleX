Refund & Cancellation Policy

This policy defines refund and cancellation rules for registrations, tournament slots, and payment disputes on BattleX.

1. Registration Cancellation

  • Cancellation requests must be submitted before the event lock/check-in time.
  • After slot lock, refunds are generally not automatic.

2. Eligible Refund Cases

  • Duplicate transaction for the same registration.
  • Payment captured but registration failed due to system error.
  • Tournament cancelled by organizer/platform before match execution.

3. Non-Refundable Cases

  • No-show, late check-in, disqualification, or rule violations.
  • User-side errors such as wrong game ID or incorrect team details after lock.
  • Losses due to gameplay outcomes or ranking results.

4. Refund Timelines

Approved refunds are processed to the original payment source, usually within 5-10 business days, depending on payment partner and banking timelines.

5. Chargeback Handling

Unauthorized chargebacks without dispute review may result in account restrictions until reconciliation is complete.

All refund approvals are subject to audit logs, match status, and anti-fraud checks.
